HOW TO USE PIX
How to use Pix
Add a pose guide
Tap Guide and pick a photo of a person. Their outline appears on the camera: drag it to move it, pinch to resize it, and line the person up with it.
Generate poses
Tap Guide › Generate poses here and take a photo of the scene. AI makes four poses for that person in that place; the one you pick becomes the guide.
Shoot together
Invite a friend from Shoot together, or give them a room code. Their phone shows your camera live, with the same guide.
From the subject's phone
The person in the photo can zoom, change the brightness and the flash, and move the guide from their own phone. Only the photographer takes the photo.
