What we heard

The brief

Pix is an Android camera app for two friends: the person in the photo sees the photographer's live view with the same pose guide and can adjust the shot. The mark had to be a P, read as a camera at a glance, and work as an app icon.

friendlyprecisesharedcamera-first

A P at first glance, a camera shutter at second; holds up from 512 px down to 32 px, with a simplified cut below that; works in one colour.

Pix — final identity2
Final design

Shutter P

The P's bowl is a camera shutter, and one of its blades runs on to become the stem.

  • Reads as a P first and a camera second: the letter and the shutter are one shape
  • The yellow aperture is the app's accent colour, and the wordmark's i is dotted with the same hexagon
  • Six straight blades on one disc and one hexagon: exact, simple geometry
  • A simplified cut (P with a hexagon opening) covers 16–24 px
